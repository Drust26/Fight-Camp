# Changelog

Each version's section is the text of its GitHub release and of the in-game update prompt.

## v1.1.0
- Automatic updates: the game checks for a new version when it starts, downloads it in the background, then
  closes, updates and reopens when you click "Restart and update" (or installs it when you quit).
- Settings shows your version and has a "Check now" button.
- The game's version number is shown on the title screen.
- Your saves stay in Documents\FightCamp\saves and are never touched by an update.

## v1.0.0
- First Windows release: installer, Fight Camp logo, saves in Documents\FightCamp\saves.
