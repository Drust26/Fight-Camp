# Changelog

Each version's section is the text of its GitHub release and of the in-game update prompt.

## v1.2.0
- Living world: AI fighters now change weight class: contenders stuck behind a champion, fighters on a losing streak
  and fighters the wrong size for their division move up or down. Each move is in the news and on the fighter's profile.
- HFC superfights between AI fighters: a champion with 3 straight defenses can move up or down to challenge the
  next division's champion. Winners become double champions, hold both belts for a few months, then keep one and
  vacate the other.
- Champion vs champion: as HFC champion, a dominant champion from the next weight class can come for your belt. Lose
  and he holds two belts. The fight gets its own poster.
- Bidding wars: when you are a sought-after free agent, your promotion and up to two rivals send offers at once.
  "Let them bid" makes them outbid each other for up to 3 rounds until each reaches its limit.
- Rival promotions poach each other's contenders, and now and then a champion leaves and vacates his belt.
- Fixed: a retiring double champion now gives up every belt he holds.
- Update notes are shown as a proper list.

## v1.1.1
- Readable save files: careers in Documents\FightCamp\saves are now plain text you can open. Older saves still load
  and switch to the readable format the next time they are saved; the previous save stays in saves\backups.
- "What's new" appears once after each update, and Settings has a "What's new" button with the full changelog.
- First update delivered through the automatic updater.

## v1.1.0
- Automatic updates: the game checks for a new version when it starts, downloads it in the background, then
  closes, updates and reopens when you click "Restart and update" (or installs it when you quit).
- Settings shows your version and has a "Check now" button.
- The game's version number is shown on the title screen.
- Your saves stay in Documents\FightCamp\saves and are never touched by an update.

## v1.0.0
- First Windows release: installer, Fight Camp logo, saves in Documents\FightCamp\saves.
